## 1. Introduction
    ### 1.1 Background
        There is a problem,getting food quick enough can be a very stressful experience.Students find themselves asking other people like their friends and roommates to get them food because they know the stress they are about to go through.Some students also go through the stress of looking online for food vendors and they may encounter scammersresturants outside school that may still delay or sometimes not available so they are left stranded.This is the daily life of Babcock students who go through the stress of looking for food because they don't want to go to Cafeteria or the Cafeteria is closed.We need to create to create a platform where students can order food and be able to pick it up on arrival to avoid long queues

    ### 1.2 Product Overview

    We are going to create software called Campus RapidBite.This platform is an online platform that will bring different resurants,food courts and shops together in a single place, easy to access by any students in their respective departments.

    The platform will let students 
    * View resturant menus
    * Select their preferred meals
    * Place orders before arriving
    * Choose a pickup time
    * Pay before arrival or at collection

## 3. Research and Findings
    ### 3.1 Research Method

    I conducted interviews with students, focused on how often students buy food from restaurants or food vendors,how often students experience long queues when buying food,how long do students usually spend waiting to order and receive food,What time do students experience the longest queues,How waiting in long queues affects students, What do students do when they see a long queue at a restaurant,Would they be interested in ordering and paying for their food before arriving at a restaurant,Which features they would like to see in a food pre-ordering app,What would be their biggest concern about using a food pre-ordering app and What improvements they would suggest for a food pre-ordering application.

    ### 3.2 Findings
    These are my findings for the interview and they are as follows:      

        My survey of  27 students showed that 77.8% of students experience long queues and 77.7% usually wait 11 minutes or longer to order and receive food.37% agreed that waiting for long frustrates them,25.9% agreed that it makes them late or lectures.When confronted with long queues 40.7% decide not to buy food,11.1% lok for another resturant whick causes losses for vendors.The longest queues are usually in with afternoon according to 37% while 29.6% say its in the evening,the rest believe that it varies.77.8% are interested in paying foor their food before arrival,18.5% are open to it while the rest are not interested

## 8. Functional Requirements

    This basically talks about what the platform should be able to do: its basic functions.

            ### 8.1 User Registration and Login
            1.The platform must recognize whether a user is a student or vendor.
            2.Users must only have access to features allowed for their roles.

            ### 8.2 Menu Access and management    
            1.Students should be able to access menus for different resturants and vendors.
            2.Resturants and Vendors must be able to upload menus.
            3.Menus must be organized by location 
            4.Students must be able to view and order food.

            2. Only approved menus should be available to students.
     
## 9. Non-Functional Requirements

    Non-functional requirements describe how well the platform should work.
        * 9.1 Performance
        Students will expect this platform to load quickly and respond to user actions without delays.
        * 9.2 Security
        This platform must protect user accounts. Users should only access information and features they are permitted to use. We have to make sure this is in place to protect student information.
        * 9.3 Usability
        This platform should have a simple design that students, and food vendors can understand without difficulty.
        * 9.4 Reliability
        This platform should work properly 
        * 9.5 Availability
        Students should be able to access the app when they have an internet connection.
        * 9.6 Compatibility
        The platform should work on supported devices so that users can access the resources without needing one specific type of device. This makes it flexible so end users (the students in question) can access them regardless of the device they are using and they must be supported.
